Cosmere RPG extensions

Owlbear Rodeo extensions for running the Cosmere RPG. Each one installs on its own.

Add one to Owlbear Rodeo

  1. In Owlbear, open your profile menu and choose Extensions.
  2. Choose Add Extension and paste the extension's manifest URL.
  3. Turn the extension on in the room. Its button appears in the toolbar.

Initiative tracker

A fast and slow turn tracker. Each round has four bands: fast PCs, fast NPCs, slow PCs, slow NPCs. Players act in any order inside their band, and the GM marks who has gone. Fast turns show two arrows for the two actions, slow turns show three.

Endeavor tracker

Success, failure and clock tracks for Discovery, Exploration, Mission, Pursuit and Legacy endeavors. The GM ticks the tracks and chooses which ones the players see.
